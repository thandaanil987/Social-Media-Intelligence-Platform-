Key Insights & Business Recommendations

What the data shows, and what to do about it. Every figure comes from the validated model.

Key insights

1. Net growth slowed in 2025

Net follower growth fell from +68,272 in 2024 to +62,557 in 2025, about 8%.

Gross gains fell 8% (90,922 to 83,435) and losses fell 8% (22,650 to 20,878), so the slowdown is mainly weaker follower acquisition, not higher churn.

2. Carousels and reels lead

Engagement per post by content type

Carousels earn the most engagement per post (5,933) and the highest engagement rate (11.40%). Reels earn the most reach per post (50,913). Text posts trail on both (5,205 and 10.71%).

3. Platforms perform about the same

Engagement per post: LinkedIn 5,689 · YouTube 5,531 · Instagram 5,466

The gap is about 4%, and engagement rates sit between 10.9% and 11.2%. Platform choice matters less than the content format.

4. Topic gap is small

Engagement per post by topic

Productivity leads (5,891) and Finance trails (5,285). The spread is about 11%, so topic is a weaker lever than format.

5. Account growth varies widely

Net growth by account

Creator_2 (Instagram, Lifestyle) gained the most (+14,129). Creator_6 (YouTube, Lifestyle) gained the least (+8,573) and also has the lowest total engagement of the 12 accounts (511,144).

6. Campaign spend is concentrated in Traffic

Budget and linked engagement by objective

Traffic holds the largest budget ($637,546 across 11 campaigns) and the most linked engagement (2.31M). Follower Growth has the smallest budget ($278,906) and the least linked engagement (801,223).

7. Engagement dipped in spring 2025

Monthly engagement rate

The rate was lowest in May 2025 (9.95%) and highest in June 2025 (11.77%). Net growth was weakest in April 2025 (+3,442).

Business recommendations

1. Shift more of the content mix toward carousels and reels

Priority: high · Basis: insights 2 and 4

Carousels and reels have the strongest engagement per post and reach per post. Text posts have the weakest on both. Run a one-quarter test that moves a share of text posts into carousels and reels, then compare engagement per post against the current mix.

2. Find the cause of the 2025 acquisition slowdown

Priority: high · Basis: insight 1 and 7

Gross gains fell 8% in 2025, which is the main driver of lower net growth. Review posting cadence, topic mix and format mix for the weakest months (especially April 2025), and set a monthly follower-gain target to track against.

3. Review the creator accounts with the weakest growth

Priority: medium · Basis: insight 5

Creator_6 has the lowest net growth and the lowest total engagement of the 12 accounts. Compare its format and topic mix with Creator_2, the strongest grower, before drawing conclusions about the creator itself, since the data doesn't show why the accounts differ.

4. Track Follower Growth campaigns against follower results, not engagement

Priority: medium · Basis: insight 6

Follower Growth campaigns produce about 2.9 linked engagements per budget dollar, compared with 3.6 to 4.0 for the other objectives. That is a proxy, not a return measure. Judge these campaigns on followers gained, which the model doesn't yet link to campaigns.

5. Fix the data gaps before drawing campaign conclusions

Priority: high · Basis: data checks

Align campaign platform with the platforms of its posts, add follower results at campaign level, confirm the budget currency, and extend the calendar beyond December 2025 to cover 2026 data.
